Loop & Row

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-25 · Describes app v1.3.2 · English storefronts only (not China App Store)

Short version

Your projects stay on your device. Loop & Row does not require an account, does not sell your data, and does not ship advertising or first-party analytics SDKs in this version. Photos and PDFs you import remain in the app on your iPhone or iPad.

Who we are

Loop & Row is an independent crochet and knitting counter app (Fiber Journal UI).

What stays on your device

  • Project names, counters, undo history, and settings
  • Pattern text you paste and checklist progress
  • Photos and PDF pattern files you choose (copied into app documents)
  • Optional JSON backups you create yourself

JSON backups do not include photo or PDF binary files. We do not operate a Loop & Row cloud sync service in v1.3.2.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or login profiles
  • No sale or brokering of personal data
  • No ads or advertising-identifier marketing
  • No first-party analytics / crash SDK claimed in product copy
  • No upload of your projects, photos, or PDFs to a Loop & Row server
  • No live in-app purchases in this build

Export & sharing

If you export JSON or use the iOS share sheet, files leave the app only because you chose to share them. Import replaces local data after confirmation. We never receive those files.

Apple & Expo

The app is built with Expo and distributed through Apple. Apple (App Store / TestFlight) and any Expo cloud tools you enable later may process device or diagnostic data under their own policies. This product is local-first; we will update this page if that changes.

Children

Loop & Row is a general craft utility, not directed at children under 13. Expected age rating: 4+.

Deletion

Uninstalling removes app data subject to iOS behavior. Settings → Delete all data erases projects on that device. We cannot remotely delete or recover local projects.

Future monetization

A later version may offer an optional one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. No live IAP exists in v1.3.2. This policy will be revised before any paid feature ships.

China storefront

We plan Individual Apple Developer enrollment and do not plan to list Loop & Row on the China App Store storefront.
